University Timetable SchedulerLatest

Desktop app that generates conflict-free university timetables for students, teachers and rooms. It models the problem with Google OR-Tools' CP-SAT solver: hard constraints like no overlaps and room capacity, plus soft preferences like fewer gaps between classes. Includes a color-coded grid view, contiguous 3-hour lab blocks, auto-saved JSON solutions and a formatted Excel export.
